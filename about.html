Why the Studio Exists

🏢 Founder & Lead AI Engineer · Jillani SofTech · Distributed · USA, UK, EU, Australia

I founded Jillani SofTech after 6 years deploying enterprise AI across all 3 major clouds. The pattern I kept running into was the same everywhere: companies buying impressive demos that never survived contact with production.

The studio has shipped 22+ production systems since — HIPAA-compliant clinical decision support for a US hospital network, autonomous financial risk agents, and agentic RAG platforms handling thousands of queries a day.

We work with regulated businesses across the US, UK, EU and Australia — RAG systems, LLM copilots, n8n automation, multi-agent architectures and MLOps infrastructure. We do not build demos. We ship production systems that pay for themselves.
