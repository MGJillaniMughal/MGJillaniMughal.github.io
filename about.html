Founder Story

Why the Studio Exists

Founder & Lead AI Engineer · Jillani SofTech · Distributed · USA, UK, EU, Australia

The same conversation kept repeating itself. A company had paid for an AI pilot, the demo had gone well, everyone had been impressed, and 9 months later nothing was in production. Not because the model was bad. Because nobody had defined what success was, legal had never been shown the data flow, and no one owned the system after handover.

I founded Jillani SofTech after 6 years of deploying enterprise AI across all 3 major clouds, specifically to work on that gap. The studio has shipped 22+ production systems since: HIPAA-compliant clinical decision support inside a US hospital network, autonomous financial risk agents, and agentic RAG platforms answering thousands of queries a day.

The method is unglamorous and it is the reason the systems stay live. Discovery ends with a metric, a baseline and written acceptance criteria. Compliance is designed into the architecture rather than bolted on before launch. Every system ships with monitoring and drift detection, because accuracy decays quietly and trust does not come back once it is gone. Across delivered work that has meant an average 47% efficiency gain and $870K+ in documented client savings.

I will also tell a client when generative AI is the wrong tool. A well-specified classical model or a plain automation pipeline is often cheaper, faster and easier to defend in an audit. Recommending the expensive option when the simple one works is how consultancies lose the second engagement.

We work with regulated businesses across the US, UK, EU and Australia: RAG systems, LLM copilots, n8n automation, multi-agent architectures and MLOps infrastructure.
